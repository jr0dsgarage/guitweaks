# guitweaks

Quality-of-life UI tweaks for World of Warcraft. Type `/guit` in game to open the settings.

## Features

### General

- Timed background behind UI error text for quick readability without lingering.
- Adjustable height for the vehicle / override action bar.
- Hide the micro menu's background and border art.
- Outline the action buttons that equip your current armor set (Retail).

### Frames

- Adjustable battleground map scale, optionally drawn beneath other frames.
- Experience, reputation and honor bars drawn above other frames.
- Party and raid frames that stay centered as the group grows and shrinks.
- Move the top center widgets (battleground scores, zone objectives) down.

### Chat

- Movable, resizable chat entry box that keeps its place relative to the screen's center.
- Hide the buttons beside the chat window, or move them to its right side.
- Per-window chat font and size.
- Stretch the main chat window over the space left for the entry box.
- Hide the chat tabs, optionally showing them on hover.

### Combat text and cooldowns

- Adjustable height and spread for the damage numbers above enemies.
- Movable scrolling combat text for damage and healing you receive.
- Cooldown timer beside the mouse when you press a spell that isn't ready.

### Other

- Movable speed panel that displays your current movement speed in real time, with customizable font, background, and border.
- Readable names above friendly players' simplified nameplates, with optional title, guild and class colors, and a friendly nameplate size.
- A colored background panel to size and place anywhere.

## In Progress

- Objective tracker enhancements are temporarily disabled while the feature is rebuilt from scratch.
